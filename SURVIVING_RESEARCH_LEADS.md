# SURVIVING_RESEARCH_LEADS

> A lead is promoted to a *tradeable* candidate ONLY after it survives all three
> gates: a complete failed-footprint denominator (Phase 4), walk-forward on
> untouched test data (Phase 5), and realistic costs (Phase 6). Nothing reaches
> that status by looking promising.

## Tradeable candidates

**NONE.**

Phase 4 tested **750 pre-registered hypotheses** across both entry conventions.
**Zero** produced a positive expectation after the verified 0.8% brokerage that
survives multiple-testing correction — before any estimated cost, tax or
slippage. 91 cohorts (open-entry) / 38 (close-entry) had a statistically real
*positive* excess over the same-day base rate, and **not one of them survived
brokerage alone.**

## Carried into Phase 5 — one family, and it is NEGATIVE

| | |
|---|---|
| Finding | A stock whose activity has **departed from its own baseline**, especially where that departure **persists**, underperforms the same-day market over the following 5–10 sessions |
| Size | excess −0.44% to −1.57% at h = 10, depending on cohort |
| Strength | t = −8 to −11, date-paired, across ~2,000 distinct dates |
| Robustness | holds under **both** entry conventions (open-entry and the close-entry variant that avoids the questionable `open` field) |
| Failure rate | ~63% of occurrences lose net of verified brokerage — a distributional tilt, **not** a rule that fires |
| Status | **UNVALIDATED** — full-sample descriptive only. No walk-forward, no holdout |

**Why it is not a trade.** Short selling is unavailable in Bangladesh, so
negative information cannot be monetised directly. Its only legitimate use is as
an **avoidance / exclusion filter** — and that decision belongs to Phase 6, after
Phase 5 shows the effect survives out-of-sample.

**Also note:** the plain rung-1 baseline (abnormal volume) carries this
information *at least as well* as the rung-2 multivariate novelty states. The
extra machinery has **not** earned its complexity, and rungs 2b–5 are not to be
built on the strength of this result.
